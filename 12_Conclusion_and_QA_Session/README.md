<h1 align="center">Conclusion and Q&A Session</h1>

This session wraps up the course with exam preparation and a Q&A. Rather than introducing new core theory, it focuses on reviewing key topics from across the semester, clarifying exam expectations, and answering remaining questions.

Use this session to revisit areas where you feel less confident, connect ideas from arithmetic, probability, statistics, linear algebra, and calculus, and prepare for the written exam. It is also an opportunity to reflect on how the mathematics covered in the course supports software engineering practice.

#### Key Concepts

- Course review across all major topic areas
- Exam preparation and expectations
- Connecting mathematics to software engineering practice
- Identifying remaining gaps in understanding

!!! tip "Learning Objectives"

    - Review the main topics covered throughout the course.
    - Identify areas that need further study before the exam.
    - Understand exam format, requirements, and expectations.
    - Connect mathematical concepts to software engineering applications.
    - Ask clarifying questions and consolidate your understanding.
