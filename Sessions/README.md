# Sessions

Click on a session in the top menu (or below) to access a plan of a specific session and additional resources for that session. A session consists of 2 x 45 minute lecture followed by 4 x 45 minutes of hands-on exercises. The students are not expected to work on the exercises at home but at the scheduled tutorials.
